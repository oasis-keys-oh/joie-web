Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add wines to my cellar?

Tap the Scan tab and point your camera at a wine label or barcode. Cavé will identify the wine automatically and add it to your collection. You can also add wines manually by tapping the + button in the Cellar tab.

What is the drink window?

The drink window shows the optimal period to open a wine. Cavé calculates this based on the wine's region, vintage, and type. Bottles in their drink window are highlighted so you always know what to open tonight.

Who is Margaux?

Margaux is your AI sommelier — Bordeaux-born, trained in private cellar management. Ask her anything about your collection, pairing suggestions, or what to open for a specific occasion. She's available in the Margaux tab.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Account → Delete Account. Your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days. This action cannot be undone.

Is my data backed up?

Yes. Your collection is stored securely in the cloud and synced across all your devices. You won't lose your data if you change phones or reinstall the app.
